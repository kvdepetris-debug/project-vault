## Priority recommendations

- **Digital art / merchandise**: Krita + Inkscape for human-editable originals; ComfyUI or InvokeAI for optional generation; Upscayl for raster enhancement. Upscaling cannot guarantee a truly sharp print-ready design; verify dimensions, artifacts, commercial model licenses and DPI at final print size.
- **Product development**: Penpot for design systems, AppFlowy for planning, Outline for documentation. Reuse existing CAD/3D printing tools from the 3D library for physical products.
- **Psychology**: PsychoPy and jsPsych for consent-based research experiments; JASP/jamovi for analysis. These are not diagnostic or therapy systems; no profiling, clinical decisions or unvalidated psychometric scores.
- **Personal finance**: Actual Budget for envelope budgeting, Firefly III for transaction-centric tracking, Ghostfolio for investments. Choose one primary finance record system rather than duplicating account data across apps.
- **Business finance**: ERPNext for integrated inventory/orders/accounting, Bigcapital or Akaunting for focused accounting, Frappe Books for lightweight bookkeeping. Evaluate sales tax, payment integrations, audit trails and accountant access before adoption.
- **Investing**: OpenBB for research; vectorbt/Backtrader/Lean for historical tests; QuantStats for risk and performance reporting. Historical backtests are not forecasts. Keep automated trading disabled and paper-only pending explicit separate authorization.

## Integration and privacy gates

1. Never put bank credentials, brokerage tokens, medical/psychology research data or business secrets into this **public** repository. This file contains links and decisions only.
2. Review licenses, maintenance, known vulnerabilities, hosting, data retention, model weight terms and commercial usage rights before any deployment.
3. Treat psychology tools as research infrastructure; obtain informed consent and handle sensitive participant data appropriately.
4. For finance tools, test with synthetic transactions first. Avoid duplicate ledgers, unintended synchronization, automated transfers, or live trades.
5. For art, check copyrights, likeness rights, print-on-demand vendor specifications, color space and physical print size.
6. Prefer evaluating one representative tool per category before deploying alternatives.

## Suggested experiments

- Art: build one print-ready fictional design using Krita, optional ComfyUI and Upscayl, then check vendor dimensions.
- Product: capture one product brief in AppFlowy, prototype in Penpot, document release in Outline.
- Psychology: run a small anonymous example reaction-time experiment in jsPsych and analyze synthetic results in JASP.
- Personal finance: import fictional monthly transactions into a disposable Actual Budget instance.
- Business finance: test fictional product sales, inventory and expenses in ERPNext versus Bigcapital.
- Investing: run a look-ahead-bias-aware historical portfolio test and review fees, slippage and drawdowns; no real orders.
